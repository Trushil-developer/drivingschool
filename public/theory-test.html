Driving Licence Process in Ahmedabad: Learner's to Permanent

Getting a driving licence in India is a two-stage process. First you apply for and pass the Learner's Licence (LL) theory test — the exact test this platform helps you prepare for. Once you hold a valid LL, you become eligible to apply for your Permanent Driving Licence (DL), which includes a practical driving test at the RTO.

A Learner's Licence is typically valid for a limited period from the date of issue, and a Permanent Driving Licence is generally valid for many years before it needs renewal — exact validity and fees are set by your state's RTO and can change, so always confirm current rules on the official Parivahan portal or at your local RTO office. What we can help with directly: passing the theory test on your first attempt, and structured driving lessons so you're ready for the practical test too.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the RTO theory test?

The RTO Learner Licence theory test has 15 multiple-choice questions. Each question has 3 options (A, B, C). You get 48 seconds per question and need at least 9 correct answers to pass.

What topics are covered?

Questions cover Traffic Rules, Road Signs, Vehicle Knowledge, First Aid, Road Safety, and Motor Vehicle Act regulations. All questions are based on the official RTO syllabus for LMV category.

Is this the same as the real RTO exam?

This mock test follows the same format, time limit and pass criteria as the actual computer-based test at the RTO office. Questions are sourced from the official RTO question bank to give you the most realistic preparation.

Do I need to create an account?

Just enter your email and verify with a one-time code. No password needed. Your mock test results and practice progress are automatically saved to your account.

What is the difference between a Learner's Licence and a Permanent Driving Licence?

A Learner's Licence (LL) is the first step — it lets you practice driving on public roads under supervision once you've passed the theory test. A Permanent Driving Licence (DL) comes after a practical driving test and lets you drive independently without restrictions. You need a valid LL before you can apply for the DL.

Can I get a driving licence without taking a test?

No — a theory test for the Learner's Licence and a practical test for the Permanent Licence are both legally required. What we can do is make sure you're genuinely prepared for both: realistic mock tests here for the theory exam, and structured lessons with our instructors for the practical one, so you pass comfortably on your first attempt.

How do I renew my driving licence?

Driving licence renewal is handled directly by your RTO, typically shortly before or after expiry, and does not involve retaking the theory or practical test in most cases. Exact steps and fees vary by state — check the Parivahan portal or visit your local Ahmedabad RTO office for the current process.
